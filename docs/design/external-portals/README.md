## Decisions to record before implementation

Each row is a proposed default. Record the approver, chosen decision and any exception in the corresponding issue before opening either implementation lane. Review Client and Candidate decisions separately. Product and security approval of applicable identity, ownership and data-sharing decisions is required even for the first implementation PR. Live exposure has additional launch gates in [security and rollback](security.md).

| ID | Decision | Proposed default | Approvers and unresolved choices |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E01 | Identity provider and isolation | Managed OIDC, distinct external issuer or isolation realm and persona-specific clients. Pick a portal-specific host and use the existing Nest process behind it. Use app-owned external sessions and a separate identity table. | Security and operations select the provider, issuer/tenant configuration, hosting boundary, data location, contract, cost and assurance claims. Confirm whether one person may have independently enrolled staff, client and candidate identities. |
| E02 | Invitation, MFA and recovery | Staff approves an exact organization/contact/email invitation. Require verified email and phishing-resistant MFA with a user-verified passkey or security key. Default invitation expiry 24 hours. Assisted recovery requires independent verification and revokes all sessions. | Security approves acceptable authenticator/recovery evidence, fallback, TTLs and support access. Product names invitation and recovery operators plus escalation owners. |
| E03 | Organization ownership | One active client organization per client identity in V1 and named individual accounts. Any active member can view submitted requests for that organization. Drafts are creator-only. Read-only organization details come from an explicit CRM field allowlist. | Product and security approve role names, shared visibility, draft handling when a member leaves, whether organization display is allowed for a prospect, and who approves membership. Org edits, delegation and switching require a follow-up. |
| E04 | Recruitment review and conversion | Explicit approve or reject, separate conversion to one internal draft mission, no client mission link. Client-facing rejection uses an approved reason code. | Recruitment and product approve required demand fields, terminology, review queue ownership and staff scope. Decide whether to introduce return-for-changes, withdrawal or cross-staff dual approval. Approve pilot service levels and rejection codes. |
| E05 | Candidate identity and ownership | Candidate self-registration creates a new self-service profile. Staff-assisted proof and review are required before linking any historical record or application. Email equality never grants access. | Product, privacy and security approve claim proof, trusted verifiers, duplicate and merge handling, conflicting claims, candidate MFA and recovery requirements. |
| E06 | Candidate projection and file policy | Versioned candidate-owned edits with staff-approved propagation, PDF CV only for the first file release, explicit status projection per owned application. | Privacy and product approve field and status allowlists, publication and notices, supported file types, malware scanning, retention, access and correction procedures. |
| E07 | Retention, audit and launch authority | Minimal payloads, restricted audit access, synthetic staging, fail-closed flags and scoped revocation drill before a separate release approval. | Privacy, security and operations approve retention periods, deletion/legal-hold process, applicable privacy obligations, incident ownership and rollout/rollback authority. |

Client V1 ends at recruitment-demand status. Anonymized candidate presentations, recruiter-mediated feedback, messaging, identity disclosure, and additional service pillars require separate approval. Candidate Portal exposes a person's own profile, documents and approved application history after its gates. Neither audience receives access to the staff CRM or the Vivier général.
