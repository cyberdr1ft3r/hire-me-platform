# Authenticated Client and Candidate Portals

Product and architecture preflight for [Issue #236](https://github.com/cyberdr1ft3r/hire-me-platform/issues/236). Both tracks are pending approval. The [runtime inventory](../audit/recruitment-lifecycle/README.md) establishes the current starting point. Public opportunities and anonymous applications work today. Hire Me staff manage client contacts, candidate records, mission presentation and feedback. The seeded `CLIENT_USER` and the `clientVisible` and `CLIENT_SHARED` fields represent foundations with the existing internal access rules. D-027 defines the sharing boundary and D-037 defers portal access to a separately approved scope. [Project decisions](../project/DECISIONS.md)

## Contracts for approval

| Contract | Proposed starting point | Approval needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| External identity | Staff-approved client invitations, verified email, short-lived single-use invite, monitored recovery, session revocation on membership removal. A separate candidate onboarding and recovery path verifies control of the account before linking records. Reuse the authentication infrastructure only if distinct external policies, session context and operational recovery can be enforced and tested. | Approve identity providers, identity and invite verifiers, recovery/MFA policy, duplicate-account handling and staff responsibilities. Resolve whether one identity can be both staff and external, or a member of multiple organizations. Unscoped access remains denied. |
| Client ownership | Each active membership binds an identity to an approved client organization. Relationship alone conveys no visibility into a mission. Resolve an active organization, its mission and an explicit presentation grant on every read, file download and feedback operation. Revoke membership or sharing on the server and invalidate any outstanding download access. | Approve membership cardinality and roles, delegated administration, and which contacts can edit company information, submit or withdraw a demand, and give feedback for a given mission. |
| Candidate ownership | After approved verification, associate the external identity with one self-service profile and explicitly linked applications. Keep staff-owned candidate and evaluation records separate. Version candidate submissions or edit requests and use staff review for fields approved for propagation. Identity collisions go to reviewed resolution with an audit trail. | Approve acceptable proof for claiming anonymous applications, handling shared or changed email addresses, reconciliation with duplicate master records, editable fields, document visibility, and ownership/deletion rules. An email match alone grants no claim. |
| Anonymized client presentation | Publish a purpose-built scoped dossier using a mission-specific presentation identifier. Allowlist role-relevant skills, qualifications, broad experience and availability. A recruiter reviews every text and derived attachment for re-identification before publication. Default exclusions include name, contact data, photos, original CVs and certificates, embedded identifiers, internal scores, notes and restricted compensation. | Approve the field allowlist, redaction policy, qualified reviewers, publication purpose and consent, expiry and revocation. Any identity release needs its own affirmative, auditable workflow and explicit approval. Pipeline movement does not authorize it. |
| Candidate data sharing | Expose candidate-owned drafts, approved documents and a defined status projection for each owned application. Maintain an allowlist for every response and file. Exclude staff evaluations, internal notes, unrelated applicants and recruiting or commercial metadata. | Approve the status vocabulary, visibility of offer/rejection information, policy for sending notices, correction workflow, and privacy/retention handling with the responsible team. |

## Workflow slices after approval

1. Define policy helpers and narrow read/write models inside the existing modular monolith. Put external endpoints behind explicit authorization, use server-side record scope, and audit membership, identity verification, access, publication, disclosure and revocation without including sensitive payloads. Give external sessions no inherited internal grants. Validate external file ownership and purpose on each download, and review local privacy and retention obligations before any rollout.
2. Pilot a client recruitment demand (`draft → submitted → under review → declined or converted`, with approved withdrawal rules). Staff decide whether a submitted demand becomes one linked mission. Keep review and document permissions independent of CRM visibility. For a mission, use `draft dossier → reviewed → published → revoked/expired`. Interest, rejection, interview requests and feedback go through Hire Me. Approve request chat separately, including participant scope and redaction across messages and notices.
3. Pilot verified candidate self-service for own profile, approved documents and own application status. Keep anonymous applications available under their current contract. Route candidate edits through the approved versioning and review flow. Keep **Recrutement**, **Formation**, and **Accompagnement** separate. Particularly sensitive personal and family support records require their own privacy design.

## Acceptance before exposure

- Test two client organizations, two authorized contacts with different scopes, two candidates and multiple missions. Verify scope for direct routes, API lookups, lists, pagination, downloads, feedback and account recovery, including revoked membership and sharing. Record audit attribution and prove sessions cannot bypass revoked scope.
- Inspect dossier payloads, documents and metadata for direct and indirect identifiers. Include rare work histories, exact employer/date combinations, free text, links, previews, notifications, messaging, and exports. A reviewed anonymous dossier must remain anonymous until an approved identity release is recorded. Never serve original identity-bearing files through dossier URLs.
- Test that candidate edits preserve staff evaluations, that each account sees only its own application statuses and files, and that ambiguous or duplicate claims require review. Exercise expiry, consent withdrawal, deletion/retention and account recovery.
- Product and privacy owners must approve identity, ownership and sharing contracts before creating implementation issues that expose external account access. Evaluate client and candidate pilots as separate releases with explicit acceptance and rollback criteria.
